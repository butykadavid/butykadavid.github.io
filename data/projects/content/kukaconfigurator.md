## KUKA Configurator (2025)

The KUKA Configurator is one of the largest professional projects I have worked on so far.

The project focuses on modernizing an existing enterprise application by gradually replacing older modules with a newer architecture. The challenge was not only building new features, but also making sure that the transition between the old and new systems remained smooth for existing users.

Even as a junior developer, I had the opportunity to take ownership of important frontend decisions and contribute to the overall direction of the application, due to my insights on modern web development frameworks. Besides implementing features, I was involved in architectural discussions, design decisions, and solving critical blockers during development.

One of the more interesting challenges was designing the authentication flow between the legacy and modernized parts of the system. The goal was to allow users to move between the old and new modules seamlessly without disrupting their existing workflows. I would share more insights, but these details are better left behind the scenes.

The project gave me valuable experience in building enterprise software where technical decisions have real impact, and where maintaining compatibility with existing systems is just as important as creating new ones.

### What I am proud of

- Contributed to the modernization of a large enterprise application.
- Took ownership of frontend architecture and design decisions.
- Helped solve critical technical challenges during development.
- Worked on authentication concepts connecting legacy and modern systems.
- Learned how to balance innovation with the practical requirements of enterprise software.
