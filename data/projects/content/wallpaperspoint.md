## WallpapersPoint (2021)

My actual first ever web project. The app was a simple image sharing site nothing more, but this was basically my introduction to modern webframeworks.
The app was a Next.js (tho I was very beginner and did not utilized on most of its strengths) application connected to a Google Firebase project. I used Firebase's storage and database to store the data and the images.

**App was shut down due to the subscription tier changes on Google Firebase. Since 2026 February, you need to upgrade your plan to have access to storage buckets**
