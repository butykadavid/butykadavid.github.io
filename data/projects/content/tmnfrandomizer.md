## TMF Randomizer (2023)

I have always liked experimenting with games that I enjoy. For example once I added skins, swapped drivers and tweaked the car performances in F1 2014 with some random modding tool. This project was my first contribution to a larger open-source Trackmania community project, that included actual coding.

The **Trackmania Forever Randomizer** is a tool that brings the very popular random map challange (RMC) and random map survival (RMS) gamemodes from Trackmania 2020 (it is also a plugin in that game) into the old Trackmania Nations/United Forever games.

The project only contained the RMC gamemode when I stared messing around with it, so I tried adding the RMS gamemode myself. Later came other ideas after also having a conversation with the author, to add presets for the settings, a private leaderboard, and some smaller changes in mechanics.

I started by just thinking about my own experience and the thought of contributing to the actual project came later. This was unfortunately noticeable in the code quality and even tho the author liked the changes, they were not approved due to bad code quality. (Also I was not very experienced at this point and I guess that was noticeable.) However he still implemented some of these ideas himself based on my work, so overall I like to think I helped the project move forward.

### What I am proud of

- Made my first contribution to a larger open-source project.
- Learned how to work with an existing codebase instead of only my own projects.
- Improved my understanding of collaborative development and community-driven software.
- Contributed to a game and community that I have enjoyed for years.
