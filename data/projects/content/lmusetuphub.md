## LMU SetupHub (2026)

LMU SetupHub is a project born from one of my biggest hobbies: motorsport and sim racing.

After spending a lot of time with **Le Mans Ultimate**, I started thinking about how difficult it is to find and compare good car setups. Usually people share setup files, but a setup alone does not really tell you much. Why was this change made? Did it actually improve lap time? Was it faster because of the setup or because of the driver?

The idea behind LMU SetupHub is to combine setup sharing with telemetry data, creating a platform where setups can be compared based on actual performance data.

The project explores the telemetry data generated by Le Mans Ultimate, which stores session information in **DuckDB** files. These files contain information such as lap times, vehicle data, telemetry channels and setup information.

The current goal is to build a website where drivers can upload their setups and share them together with the data behind them.

The project uses:

- **Next.js**
- **TypeScript**
- **Tailwind CSS**
- **shadcn/ui**

## What I am proud of

- I managed to reverse engineer and explore the telemetry format used by Le Mans Ultimate.
- I created a concept that combines two things I really enjoy: software development and motorsport.
