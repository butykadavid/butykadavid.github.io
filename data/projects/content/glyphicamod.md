## Glyphica: Typing Survival - Language Learning Mods (2026)

While playing **Glyphica: Typing Survival**, I started thinking: since the whole game is based around typing words, why not use it to practice another language as well?

So I created two Steam Workshop mods:
- Hungarian → German language learning pack
- German → English language learning pack

The idea is simple: instead of only displaying one word, the mods show the original language together with the target language, allowing players to practice vocabulary while playing the game.

The technical side was interesting, as I had to understand how the game's localization and word systems work in order to create a custom language learning experience.

The mods were also used by other players, reaching **200+ visitors and 100+ subscribers** combined on the Steam Workshop.

- Created something that turns a normal game mechanic into a small learning tool.
- Published my first Steam Workshop mods.
- Made something simple that was actually useful for other people.
