## This very webiste (2022)

This website served as my personal portfolio for many years at this point. But than in July 2026 I decided to polish it, refactor it, use it to learn a bit more about web components, as I've been developing React for years, but I felt like I have a lot to learn about whats under the hood.

In the same time I felt like the previous way of showcasing projects (big cards with images) kind of prevented me from showing off my smaller projects, experimental stuff or non-project related personal growth.

Also I decided to start a **blog** in this safe place, as I thought it would be interesting to have an archive about my thoughts and it could motivate me to learn and train my brain more seriously.

### But then came a problem
This site is a basic static website that is hosted with the default Github Pages. That means you have no backend and it would be really tiring and boring to manage a blog by only just pushing .md files to the repo. 

### Solution
So I created a small **express.js** backend app, that I am hosting on a separate site, and that can communicate with the current app. **This way I can manage content from this site, and you can see it**.

### What I am proud of
- I finally gave purpose to this personal site of mine.
- I created a small archive of my progress as a developer, my personal blog.
- With a workaround I managed to bring this site to a next level
