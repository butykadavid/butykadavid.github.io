## GameNotes (2023)
GameNotes is basically my first more serious hobby project, after my early steps in web development with the WallpapersPoint project. At this time I was already studying at uni, but I was still lacking academic or professional experiences with modern web development frameworks. So once again I was learning the stack on my own.
The project uses **Next.js** and **Google Firebase**.
Now to describe what the project is, I bring you the first paragraph of its *About* page:

> GameNotes is a video game review site that encourages objective rating of videogames, by providing seven main aspects to rate for each game. Due to this system, the final ratings does not necessarily represent how good games are overall, but rather how well designed they are from an artistic point of view.

In short, it is a website to share opinions about videogames. It also provides some information about some current gaming deals.
