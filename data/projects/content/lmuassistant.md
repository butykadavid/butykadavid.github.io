## LMU Racing Assistant (2026)

At some point I realized that I spend a lot of time looking at racing data, so of course the next logical step was to build my own race engineer.

This project was created as part of a university course, where our team of five had to take an idea from concept to a working MVP. I was responsible for managing the team and coordinating the development process (adn developing myself too).

The idea was to create a racing assistant for **Le Mans Ultimate** that could analyze live race data and provide useful information while driving. Instead of just showing raw numbers, the goal was to turn the data into actual decisions: helping with pit strategy, understanding race situations, and providing useful information through an overlay.

After races, the collected data could be analyzed to understand the bigger picture: where time was lost, how the whole grid performed, and what could be improved.

Besides the technical development, we also had to approach the project like a real product. We talked with **potential users**, **analyzed the market**, **created strategies**, **defined MVPs**, and **worked with KPIs** to measure progress.
