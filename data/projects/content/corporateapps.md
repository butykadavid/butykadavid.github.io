## Corporate Applications

During my professional career, I have been working on enterprise applications in a full-stack development environment.

Most of my daily work revolves around **C#/.NET** on the backend and **React** on the frontend, building and maintaining applications that are used in a real business environment. Unlike hobby projects, where I can freely experiment with any direction, professional software comes with different challenges: existing systems, business requirements, reliability, maintainability, and collaboration with other developers.

Working on these applications taught me a lot about writing software that is not only functional, but also understandable and sustainable in the long term.

Besides the technical side, I also gained experience working in an agile environment, collaborating with other developers, discussing requirements, reviewing solutions, and continuously improving the way we build software.

### What I am proud of

- Developed full-stack applications using modern web technologies.
- Gained experience working with real-world business requirements and constraints.
- Learned how important maintainability, communication, and good architecture decisions are in larger software projects.
- Improved not only as a developer, but also as a team member working in a professional environment.
